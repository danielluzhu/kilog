Prilepin's Table

A classic guideline for how much volume to do at a given intensity (% of 1RM), from Soviet weightlifting research. It was developed for Olympic-style lifting — it applies most directly to Complex lifts (snatch, clean, jerk) and other big Compound barbell lifts, and doesn't really have anything to say about Isolation work.
