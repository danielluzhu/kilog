Write the date once, then one line per exercise below it: 

2026-07-30
FSQ 135x5, 140x5, 145x3
BP 95x8, 100x6

A spreadsheet-style row (date, exercise, set1, set2, ...) also works, and either style can be mixed in the same paste. Sets can be separated by a comma, a tab, or just a space — all three are treated the same. Unrecognized exercise abbreviations are added to the dictionary automatically, same as logging one at a time.

A set can name the equipment's own weight, added on top of what's plated: S+61x12 is a machine's sled plus 61kg, S25+61x12 spells out a 25kg sled for machines that differ from the exercise's usual one, and B+30x8 is the 20kg barbell plus 30kg, for landmine work.
