Weighted fatigue (WFU)

The same sets, priced by what they cost: x2 clean & jerk, x1.5 snatch, clean, jerk and their pulls, x1 compound lifts, x0.4 isolation. Technique drills and steady-state cardio don't score; intervals count at x1. Bar height is fatigue cost, not set count — so a week of accessory work sits well below a week of the same number of Olympic sets.
