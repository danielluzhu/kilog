A week built around the snatch and the clean & jerk, with the weighted pull-up and weighted dip as the second priority and one or two runs/hikes for aerobic base. Each of those five movements gets a heavy, a medium and a light exposure across the week, so nothing is trained hard twice in a row and nothing goes a week without being touched. Accessories never get a heavy day — that's the point of calling them accessories. Prescribed weights are computed from your own logged estimated 1RMs, and every number on this page is editable.

Why the week is shaped this way

MovementHeavyMediumLight
  • Heavy / medium / light per movement, not per week. The snatch, the clean & jerk, the squat, the pull-up and the dip each get three exposures — one that pushes the top end, one that does the work, one that keeps the pattern fresh without adding fatigue. Frequency is what drives Olympic technique; intensity is what you rotate. The grid above is built from the plan itself, so an edit that leaves a movement without one of the three shows up as a dash.
  • At least 48h between two heavy exposures of the same pattern. Heavy snatch Monday, medium Friday, light Saturday. Heavy pull-up and dip Tuesday, medium and light at the back of the week. Heavy clean & jerk lands Friday, four days off the heavy snatch.
  • Two full rest days — Thursday breaks the week in half right before the heaviest session, and Sunday closes it. Thursday will take an easy hike; it will not take a lift.
  • Squats are back-loaded, not front-loaded. The heavy front squat sits on the same day as the heavy snatch — one hard leg day, not two — and the back squat comes in at medium after the heavy clean & jerk, when it can't blunt a pull.
  • The runs stay easy. One conversational 30–40 min run midweek and one longer hike or easy run Saturday. Aerobic base costs nothing if it stays aerobic; intervals on a lifting week compete with the squats.
  • The week type waves across a month. Heavy → medium → heavy → deload keeps average weekly load near what you've actually been absorbing, instead of running the top week every week.

Customizing this page

Customize turns every day into an editable form: change the sets, reps and intensity band, swap an exercise for any abbreviation in the dictionary, drop a slot, add a slot, add a day or make a day a rest day. Edits save to this browser as you make them and survive a reload — Reset to default throws them away. Copy as JSON and Paste JSON move a plan between devices.

Each slot's intensity is a percentage band of an estimated 1RM. By default that's the exercise's own best, but a slot can be pegged to a different lift — the snatch high pull is written as a percentage of the snatch, the pause front squat as a percentage of the front squat — so the accessory follows the lift it serves. Bodyweight movements are prescribed as added weight (+41 kg), matching how they're logged.

To change the default that Reset restores, edit DEFAULT_PLAN at the top of public/weekly.js.
