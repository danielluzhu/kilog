Map the shorthand codes used in the log (e.g. FSQ) to their full exercise names (e.g. Front Squat). Entries with no name yet were found in the imported log and are waiting to be filled in. The Type column flags compound lifts and, when the name says more than just the base movement (e.g. "Front Squat", "Tall Snatch"), marks them as a variation. The Equipment column distinguishes bodyweight (pull-ups, dips — including weighted), free weight (barbell/dumbbell/kettlebell), cable, and machine exercises. The Fatigue column buckets by rough systemic cost per set: Complex (snatch, clean, jerk — technical and most fatiguing), Compound (squats, deadlifts, presses, pulls), or Isolation (single-muscle accessory work).
